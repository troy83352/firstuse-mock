WASH RULES

The clock follows the rule that applies to you.

96 hours

Idaho and Texas. Texas rule is 25 TAC Â§217.64: if more than 96 hours pass after cleaning and sanitizing, and before first use, the tank must be re-sanitized.

72 hours

New Mexico, Grade A PMO via 21.34.3.8 NMAC. Most other PMO states follow 72. Confirm the rule for each state you haul in.

Tag kept 15 days

Texas requires the removed wash tag to be kept at the wash location for at least 15 days. First Use keeps the photo with the record.

Rules change. Confirm hours with your regulator or your plant before you rely on them.
